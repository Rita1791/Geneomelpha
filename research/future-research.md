# 🔬 Future Research & Development Roadmap

> *"Today's prototype. Tomorrow's precision medicine platform."*

Geneomelpha was developed with a long-term vision of transforming complex genomic reports into understandable, clinically meaningful insights for patients, clinicians, researchers, and healthcare professionals.

While the current version demonstrates the concept of accessible genomic interpretation, the future roadmap focuses on expanding Geneomelpha into an AI-assisted, clinically relevant, and research-driven bioinformatics platform.

# 🟢 Phase 1 — Foundation (Current)

## Current Capabilities

- Patient-friendly genomic report interpretation
- Interactive web-based platform
- Clinical report generation
- QR-code verification
- Support for multiple genomic service modules
- Clean and intuitive user interface

### Goal

Build an accessible genomic interpretation platform that bridges the gap between computational genomics and non-specialist users.

---

# 🟣 Phase 3 — Clinical Genomics Expansion

## Research Focus

Expand Geneomelpha into a more comprehensive clinical genomics platform.

### Planned Features

- ClinVar evidence integration

- Rare disease prioritization
- Carrier screening enhancement

### Expected Impact

✔ Improved clinical relevance
