# 🧬 Genelpha

### Making Clinical Genomics Understandable for Everyone

Genelpha is a modern bioinformatics platform that transforms complex genomic reports into intuitive, patient-friendly summaries.

It bridges the communication gap between genomic laboratories and people without a medical or biological background.

## 🌐 Live Demo
https://genelpha.netlify.app/

## Why Genelpha?

Advances in genomic sequencing have made genetic testing increasingly accessible.

However, genomic reports remain highly technical and difficult to interpret for:

- Patients
- Families
- Medical students
- Primary care physicians
- Healthcare professionals without genetics training
Genelpha was developed to bridge this communication gap by transforming technical genomic reports into intuitive visual summaries while preserving scientific accuracy.

## Scientific Impact

Genelpha demonstrates how bioinformatics can be translated into an accessible clinical application.

Instead of displaying raw genomic information, the platform presents clinically meaningful interpretations in simple language that can be understood by non-specialists.

The project illustrates how computational biology can support patient-centred precision medicine and improve genomic literacy.
